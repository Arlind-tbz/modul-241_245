# Speaker Notes: Parkplatz Sharing Zürich

---

## Slide 1: Title Slide (Parkplatz Sharing Zürich)
- **Goal**: Hook the audience immediately.
- **Hook**: "Everyone here who drives a car in Zurich knows the pain of the 6:00 PM search."
- **Intro**: "This is our solution for a smarter, peer-to-peer (P2P) parking network in our city."

## Slide 2: Der Status Quo (01-einstieg.md)
- **Context**: "Zurich has over **134,000 registered cars**—and that's just the tip of the iceberg."
- **The Pain**: "Every evening, thousands of residents waste time and fuel circling the block. It’s an everyday frustration that feels unnecessary."

## Slide 3: Analyse (02-problem-definition.md)
- **Irony**: "While you circle the block, **private garages are sitting empty**. The problem isn't a lack of space; it's a lack of connectivity."
- **The Gap**: "There’s no easy way to book these private spots. We have no overview, no contact, and no spontaneous booking system."
- **Transition**: "➔ This is an information and mediation problem."

## Slide 4: Fakten & Insights (03-fakten.md)
- **Market Opportunity**: "70% of private garages are unutilized. That is 'dead capital'."
- **Financial Incentive**: "Owners can earn up to **CHF 40.-/day** just by sharing their unused space."
- **Validation**: "We’ve identified 8 key quarters in Zurich where this problem is critical."

## Slide 5: Bestehende Alternativen (04-kontext.md)
- **Comparison**:
  - **Parking Houses**: Too expensive and detached from residential zones.
  - **EasyPark**: Only manages public blue/white zones.
  - **Google/Search**: No real-time booking for private property.
- **The Lücke**: "No existing solution connects private providers directly and in real-time."

## Slide 6: Die Design-Challenge (05-design-challenge.md)
- **Our Vision**: "How might we bring drivers and owners together so that booking is **Fast, Local, and P2P**?"
- **The Pillars**: "It has to be: **Uncomplicated** (3 clicks), **Affordable** (market-driven), and **Trustworthy**."

## Slide 7: Unsere Lösung (06-call-to-action.md)
- **Product Showcase**: "This is our interactive map. It makes private parking 'visible'."
- **User Journey**: "Select a spot, set the duration, and confirm. 3 clicks. No registration hurdle."
- **Benefit**: "It’s better for the owner, cheaper for the user, and reduces search traffic in neighborhoods."

## Slide 8: Abschluss & Fragen (07-q-and-a.md)
- **Demo**: "You can try the prototype yourself right now at **parking.sulej.ch**."
- **Closing**: "Thank you for your attention. A special thanks to class **pe22a & pe22b** for the feedback!"
- **QA**: "Now, I’m happy to answer any questions."
